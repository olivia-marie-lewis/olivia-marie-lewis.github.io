title: "Enhancing Access to Methadone Treatment in Three HEALing Communities Study Sites: A Spatial and Geostatistical Study"
excerpt: "The aim of this project was to use network analysis and descriptive mapping to evaluate how walk and drive-time access to methadone treatment, medication to reduce risk of fatal opioid overdose, changed following the opening of new treatment facilities across multiple communities in Massachusetts. I juxtaposed walk and drive-time buffers with opioid overdose data and determined rates of fatal overdose by race and ethnicity to assess geographic access for different populations. Our analyses revealed that all new treatment centers increased drive-time access for populations in need of treatment, but opening centers in dense downtown areas can greatly expand walk-time access for diverse groups.
